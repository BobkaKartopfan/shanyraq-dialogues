Our name

In a Kazakh home, the shanyraq is the crown of the yurt, the circle at the top that holds the structure together. It stands for family, continuity, and a shared roof. Shanyraq Dialogues takes its name from that image: a place where people gather under one roof and talk.

Our purpose

Shanyraq Dialogues is a discussion platform organized by the Embassy of Kazakhstan in Belgium. It brings Kazakhstan and Europe into direct conversation, with the aim of building mutual understanding and stronger ties. Each session puts a single topic on the table and invites people who work on it: representatives of EU institutions and governments, experts and researchers, journalists, business and cultural figures, and civil society.

Topics

The format is deliberately open. Sessions are built around real discussion rather than speeches, and the topics range from political and constitutional reforms to education and science cooperation, tourism, and how Kazakhstan is perceived in Europe. The platform launched in December 2025, and sessions have since taken place in Brussels, including one held inside a traditional yurt.
