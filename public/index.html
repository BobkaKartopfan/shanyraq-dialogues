Foundation

Mission, Values, and Goals

Mission

Build Mutual Understanding

Bring Kazakhstan and Europe into direct conversation. In Brussels, Shanyraq Dialogues connects policymakers, experts, researchers, journalists, and civil society to explore Kazakhstan's development and the issues that shape the partnership.

Values

Openness, Respect, Trust

Create space for questions, different perspectives, and first-hand experience. Open discussion and cultural exchange help participants look beyond stereotypes, understand one another, and build trust.

Goals

Connect Dialogue to Cooperation

Identify shared interests in public policy, education, science, tourism, and culture. Connect people and institutions, encourage the exchange of ideas, and open up opportunities for lasting cooperation between Kazakhstan and Europe.
