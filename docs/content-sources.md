# Shanyraq Dialogues: homepage foundation copy

Researched on 18 November 2026. Applies to the Mission, Values, and Goals cards in `public/index.html`.

The cards are original editorial summaries of the platform's stated purpose and documented activities. They are not quotations from an officially adopted mission statement or values charter. Goals describe intended cooperation, rather than claiming that the platform has delivered particular agreements or projects.

## Primary sources

- [Kazakhstan Ministry of Foreign Affairs: platform launch](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/mfa/press/region-news/details/28186?lang=en). Describes an open forum bringing government representatives, experts, and civil society into substantive discussion, with mutual understanding and trust as its purpose. Supports the Mission card and the emphasis on questions and open exchange in Values.
- [Embassy of Kazakhstan in Belgium: travel and culture discussion](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/mfa-brussels/press/news/details/1224852?lang=en). Documents participation by EU institutions, media, tourism professionals, and cultural figures; discussion of personal travel experiences and stereotypes; and the platform's role in developing political, economic, cultural, and public ties. Supports Values and the cultural and tourism themes in Goals.
- [Kazakhstan Ministry of Foreign Affairs: knowledge economy roundtable](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/mfa/press/region-news/details/29542?lang=en). Documents participation by universities, research centres, experts, diplomats, and the European External Action Service, with discussion of scientific and educational cooperation. Supports the research audience in Mission and the education and science themes in Goals.
